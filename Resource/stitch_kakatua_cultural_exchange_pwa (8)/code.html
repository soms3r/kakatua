Be Kind

Kindness is our currency. We encourage positive communication, active empathy, and constructive feedback. Think of how your words land before you speak.

Respect Others

Our flock comes from many lands. Practice cultural sensitivity and respect personal privacy. No doxxing, harassment, or non-consensual sharing of data.

Moderation Policy

To maintain order, we follow a strict "Three-Strike" policy for minor infractions:

  • 1 Formal warning & educational resource
  • 2 48-hour temporary nest suspension
  • 3 Permanent removal from the flock
