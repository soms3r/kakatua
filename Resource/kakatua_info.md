# Kakatua: Project Overview

## Mission
"Everyone belongs here." 

Kakatua is an open-source, volunteer-led language and culture exchange platform[cite: 1]. We foster meaningful global connections through purposeful language practice and authentic cultural discovery, moving beyond the limitations of random chat apps[cite: 1].

## Core Philosophy
- **Community-First:** A space for learning, respect, and curiosity[cite: 1].
- **No Toxic Loops:** We avoid popularity metrics and dating-style matching[cite: 1].
- **Safety:** A tiered moderation system protects the community[cite: 1].

## Features
- **Find a Kakatua:** Smart matching based on language goals, timezones, and interests[cite: 1].
- **Build My Nest:** Mandatory profile setup and "Culture Card" creation before entering video chat[cite: 1].
- **Discover a Culture:** Structured yet flexible cards to share traditions, food, and history[cite: 1].
- **Polishing My Wings:** Real-time learning tools and grammar suggestions during calls[cite: 1].
- **My Daily Flights:** A mission-based system to reward consistency and help others[cite: 1].
- **Alert the Flock:** Community-driven moderation to handle reports[cite: 1].

## Technical Stack (PWA Strategy)
- **Frontend:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui[cite: 1].
- **Backend:** PostgreSQL (Database), Auth.js (Authentication), Node.js/FastAPI[cite: 1].
- **Video:** P2P WebRTC signaling (cost-effective and scalable)[cite: 1].
- **Hosting:** Vercel (Frontend) & Railway/Render (Backend)[cite: 1].

## Moderation Policy
We enforce a strict "three-strike" policy for community reports[cite: 1]:
1. **5 Reports:** 15-day suspension[cite: 1].
2. **10 Reports:** 30-day suspension[cite: 1].
3. **20+ Reports:** Permanent global ban (User ID + IP address)[cite: 1].

## Roadmap
- **Phase 1:** UI design, "Build My Nest" onboarding, and Auth[cite: 1].
- **Phase 2:** Matching engine ("Find a Kakatua") and P2P video calls[cite: 1].
- **Phase 3:** Culture Cards, Grammar tools, and Mission system[cite: 1].
- **Phase 4:** Community scaling and PWA optimization[cite: 1].
